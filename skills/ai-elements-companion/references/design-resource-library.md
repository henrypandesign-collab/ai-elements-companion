# Design Resource Library

Curated public references, collected 2026-11-20. Follow shortlisted links before relying on current coverage, code availability or licensing. Some LinkedIn entries use original short links with unverified destinations.

## AI design pattern libraries

- [Design Patterns For Agentic UX and AI Products (Vitaly Friedman)](https://lnkd.in/dpmdSYmg) — Read the overview for onboarding, chat, input/output, collaboration, audio and agentic UX examples.

### General AI interaction
- [The Atlas of AI Interaction Design (Brandon Harwood)](https://ai-interaction.com) — Discover AI interaction models and examples.
- [GenAI UX Design Patterns (Sharang Sharma)](https://lnkd.in/dbjQtcUD) — Find generative AI UX patterns.
- [AI Interaction Design Patterns (Emily Campbell, Shape of AI)](https://www.shapeof.ai) — Find input, steering, controls, trust, inline actions, plans and confirmations.
- [AIverse Design Patterns for AI (Kshitij Agrawal, Dave Brown and Clair Sun)](https://lnkd.in/dBj86jHx) — Browse cross-cutting AI design patterns.
- [AI UX Patterns (Luke Bennis)](https://lnkd.in/dF9AZeKZ) — Browse AI UX patterns.
- [AI UX Playground](https://aiuxplayground.com/patterns) — Browse searchable examples for trust, chat, agents, and input/output, including demos where available.
- [AI Design Patterns Catalogue (Maggie Appleton)](https://lnkd.in/ebAp9Sb8) — Browse a catalog of AI design patterns.

### Agentic UX and trust
- [Agentic UX Patterns (Dustin Kirk)](https://lnkd.in/d3WKrZnW) — Design agent workflows and interactions.
- [Design Patterns For Building Trust (Projects by IF)](https://lnkd.in/etZ7mm2Y) — Explore patterns that help users decide when to trust an interaction.

### Voice, audio and multimodal
- [Voice AI UX Patterns (via Sharang Sharma)](https://lnkd.in/dNCUTART) — Find voice AI interaction patterns.
- [Voice of AI (Anne Cantera)](https://www.voiceofai.io) — Explore design guidance for voice, chat, multimodal and agentic systems.
- [AI Audio Guidelines (Harsheen Vohra)](https://lnkd.in/dQFcg3Vu) — Review AI audio experience guidelines.

### AI entry points and prompts
- [Where should AI sit in your UI? (Sharang Sharma)](https://lnkd.in/dyyMKuU9) — Decide where to put an AI capability in an interface.
- [Prompt UX Patterns (Sharang Sharma)](https://lnkd.in/dWE8Ypbr) — Design prompt entry and input interactions.

## Supporting design and implementation resources

### Product screens and interaction states
- [Refero](https://refero.design/) — Research real product screens, layouts and visual treatments.
- [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) — Research real flows and AI or mobile screens.
- [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/) — Find React and shadcn component candidates.
- [Transitions.dev](https://transitions.dev/) — Study microinteractions, loading, thinking and state transitions.
- [Beautiful UI](https://beautifului.dev/) — Compare AI-native work surfaces such as prompt bars, approval, streaming, context cards and structured results.
